## ***Idea***

### Inspiration

Traffic Lights are signalling devices positioned at road intersections, pedestrian crossings, and other locations to control flows of traffic:

- The green light allows traffic to proceed in the direction denoted, if it is safe to - do so and there is room on the other side of the intersection;
- The red signal prohibits any traffic from proceeding;
- The yellow light warns that the signal is about to change from green to red or from red to green. 



To apply a policy of Test/Trace/Quarantine facing COVID-19 we need a similar control logic for our daily activities.

## ***Solution***

### Detail

1. Yellow Light Status:  Everyone goes through a Test x 2 within 48 hrs from certain authorized agencies to determine their next 2 Weeks social status, information will be recorded in QR Code and assign to each individual.

2. Green Light Status:  Once test negative, you are assigned a Green QR pass, you can go out and travel, pass will be expired in 2 Weeks, then you will need a new test to renew your pass.

3. Red Light Status: Once test positive, you are assigned a Red QR pass, you can not go out or travel, please stay home, rest and cooperate with doctors to recovery, this status is expired in 2 Weeks and you can require new test on demand after that.
